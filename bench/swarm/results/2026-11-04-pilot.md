## Verdict

**NO-GO.** The pilot gate fails on four independent criteria: text lift is
+1 case (gate: +2) with regressions present; modality lift is negative
(0/10 vs solo-VL 3/4 on vision); decline recall is 0.80 with false-answer
0.20 (gate: 1.00 / 0) and the d1-only baseline false-accepts out-of-scope;
contract violations are 44 on the candidate (gate: 0); judge ECE 0.54
(gate ≤ 0.10, in-sample). The same-model control (7/14) beats the candidate
team (4/24) on overlapping cases, so extra same-model calls do not rescue
it — exactly the PRD's stop condition.

## Next thread

The thread is **not closed**; the next thread is the echo-envelope contract,
not more team shape: the 1.7B at temperature 0.1 cannot reliably echo the
four-block IDENTITY/CAPACITIES/DECISION/BODY envelope (44 violations, mostly
`output_schema_unknown`/`identity_missing`/`citation_missing`), so move text
roles to stamp mode (adapter stamps identity/capacity, model returns only
DECISION+BODY) or add constrained decoding, then re-run `text-lite` and
`team-full` on the 14 text-subset cases. In parallel, the perception
sufficiency gate (0.30) rejects every audio perception (d1 scores 0.03–0.07)
— either the gate or the perception prompt is wrong, and the d1-only
false-accept on O1/O2 needs the scope answer wired to a decline. Until the
envelope contract holds, no team-shape number is meaningful.
